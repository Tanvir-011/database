4. INSERT Trigger

শুধু INSERT হলে execute করবে।

CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER trg_insert_emp
AFTER INSERT ON employee
FOR EACH ROW
BEGIN
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('New employee inserted');
END;
/

তারপর:

INSERT INTO employee
VALUES (102, 'Karim', 25000);

Output:

New employee inserted
